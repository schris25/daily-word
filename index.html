The coloured bars show each weekday's level, from Monday (easy) to Sunday (brutal). Past puzzles do not change your streak or statistics.

How to play

Guess the five-letter word. There is one new word every day.

After each guess the tiles change colour:

  • A The letter is in the word and in the right place.
  • B The letter is in the word but in a different place.
  • C The letter is not in the word.

Harder every day

Monday is the easiest and Sunday the hardest: the words get trickier each day of the week.

  • Monday You get a 7th guess.
  • Sunday Hard rules: letters you have found must be used in every later guess.

Missed a day? Open Past puzzles (the calendar button) to play any earlier puzzle.

Want more? A Practice round gives a random word at a random level. Past puzzles and practice rounds do not count towards your statistics.
