Daily Word

How to play

Guess the five-letter word in six tries. There is one new word every day.

After each guess the tiles change colour:

  • A The letter is in the word and in the right place.
  • B The letter is in the word but in a different place.
  • C The letter is not in the word.

Finished today's word? Use Practice round to keep playing. Practice games do not count towards your statistics.
